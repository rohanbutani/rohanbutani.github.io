Another open thread How does adaptation propagate through multi-agent systems? +

An early experiment I’m considering: let small language-model agents solve reasoning tasks and vote on answers, then adapt one agent. Measure changes in accuracy, consensus, and robustness across communication topologies. The question is how a local change alters a collective system—and how that effect scales.
